OpenFinances privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · OpenFinances 1.0.15 and later

OpenFinances is a personal finance app for Android, made by OpenProject. This policy says what it does with your data, in the same words the app uses in its own Privacy section

Nothing leaves your phone

OpenFinances has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet, so Android won't let it go online

Your data

Your expenses, income, budgets, debts and settings stay only in the app's storage on this phone, unless you turn on Android backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them

Backups

A backup is made only when you ask for one: a file saved where you choose. Where it goes from there is up to you. Android's own backup to your Google account is off unless you turn on Encrypted Android backup in Settings, under Data. On, Android copies the app's data only when it encrypts it with your screen lock, so Google can't read it. Off, Android's own phone-to-phone transfer leaves the data out too, though some phone makers' transfer tools may still copy it

Bank messages and notification access

Off unless you turn it on. Then Android asks you to allow notification access, so the app can read the notifications your messaging app shows for new texts and suggest entries for bank transactions. Only messages recognized as bank transactions are kept; every other notification is ignored. Nothing is sent anywhere

Update check

Only when you tap Check for updates in About, Google Play (not this app) looks for a newer version; Google receives the app's version and device details

Fingerprint lock

Your fingerprint is checked by Android itself; the app never sees it

Rating the app

Now and then, after you save entries, Google Play may ask you to rate the app. The app only counts saves on this phone. A rating goes to Google Play, never to the app

Keeping your data safe

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone, which Android keeps from other apps, and the app can lock itself behind your fingerprint. The app never sends your data to us, so we hold none of it

Deleting your data

Delete entries in the app, or uninstall it to delete everything it keeps. Backup files you saved are yours to delete. A copy in your Google account's backup, made with Encrypted Android backup on or by a version before 1.0.15, is managed in your Google account's backup settings: the app can't delete it

Earlier versions

Before 1.0.15 the app had no Encrypted Android backup setting: if Android's backup was on in your phone's settings, Android could include the app's data in your Google backup. Updating the app may not remove such a copy: delete it in your Google account's backup settings

Contact

OpenFinances is made by OpenProject. For any question about privacy, email hs.96@outlook.com

Changes
