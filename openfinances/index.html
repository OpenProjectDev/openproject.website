01Privacy

No internet permission

The app has no internet permission, no account and no server, so Android itself keeps it offline

Check it yourself

Every permission an Android app has is declared in its manifest. OpenFinances’ manifest is public on GitHub, and the internet isn’t in it

Read the manifest →Read the privacy policy →

02Features

Everything your money needs. Nothing it doesn’t

Record

Everything that comes in and goes out, in a few taps

Every entry

Record expenses and income, each with a category, a date and a note

Bank messages

If you allow notification access, the app can read your bank’s messages from their notifications and offer them for recording. Show it a message’s layout once and it knows the next one. This is optional, and the reading happens on your phone

Statistics

See where your money goes: categories, daily spending, spending pace and a calendar of your days

Comparisons

Compare this period with the last one and with your own average

Plan

Budgets, bills ahead and debts, in one place

Made to fit you

03Testing

Help us launch

OpenFinances is in closed testing on Google Play, and it needs testers before it can launch. If you have an Android phone and a Google account, you can help:

  1. 1

    Sign in to Google, then join the testers group

    groups.google.com/g/openfinances
  2. 2

    Opt in to the test, with the same Google account

    play.google.com/apps/testing/…
  3. 3

    Install OpenFinances from Google Play, and stay opted in

    Google Play

Using the app normally is all it takes. Bugs and ideas are welcome on GitHub. Open an issue
