Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy covers the website openproject.website and its pages on subdomains, such as openfinances.openproject.website. Each OpenProject app has its own privacy policy for the app itself.

No cookies, no tracking

These sites set no cookies, run no analytics or ads, and have no forms or accounts. Their fonts and images are served from the sites themselves.

Hosting

The sites are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. GitHub Privacy Statement

Links to other sites

Links to other sites, such as GitHub or Google Play, lead to services with their own privacy policies.
